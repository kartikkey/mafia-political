Genre: Multiplayer social deduction

A fast-paced multiplayer game where ordinary MPs try to keep Parliament functioning while undercover Deep State

HACKATHON PRINCIPLE: Build one complete, fun match before adding extra roles or complex

 1. Game Overview
 NETAGIRI is a multiplayer social-deduction game set inside a fictional Indian Parliament. Players are secretly
 assigned to one of two factions: Government or Deep State. Government players complete parliamentary tasks and
 identify infiltrators. Deep State agents blend in, sabotage operations and eliminate government MPs.

 The core experience combines the movement and task loop of an impostor game with the discussion, deception and
 voting of Mafia. The political setting gives the game its own personality through parliamentary rooms, emergency
 sessions and breaking-news announcements.

 The central question: Who is working for the country, and who is secretly trying to bring down the
 government?

 Design goals
 • Immediately understandable to first-time players.
 • Fun with 6–10 people in a single match.
 • Short rounds with frequent moments of suspicion and debate.
 • A recognizable Parliament setting, not just a generic map reskin.
 • Small enough to build, test and demo within eight hours.


 2. Story & Setting
 India is facing a period of political instability. Parliament is in session and critical government operations must
 continue. Unknown to most MPs, an underground organization called the Deep State has infiltrated Parliament. Its
 agents are disguised as ordinary politicians. They intend to destabilize the government, eliminate influential MPs and
 bring parliamentary operations to a standstill.

 The remaining MPs must complete their duties, investigate suspicious activity, report eliminated colleagues and vote
 out suspected infiltrators. Every player looks like a politician. No one can identify an agent by appearance alone.

 4. Parliament Map
 Use one connected top-down map with five recognizable areas. These can be sections of one scene rather than
 separate levels. Keep corridors short and routes easy to understand.

  Area                              Gameplay purpose

  Lok Sabha                         Emergency meetings, discussion and voting. The Speaker's chair is the focal point.

  Prime Minister's Office           Administrative tasks such as approving government documents or passing a bill.

  Finance Ministry                  Budget and financial tasks, such as verifying allocations or records.

  Records Room                      Organizing files, retrieving missing documents and verifying records.

  Central Hall                      Infrastructure tasks such as restoring power or repairing communications.


 Map implementation guidance
 • Build a single tile-based or image-based map with connected rooms.
 • Add clear labels or signs for each area.
 • Use walls and furniture as collision obstacles.
 • Place task stations at visible, easy-to-reach points.
 • Keep the Lok Sabha central enough that players can reach meetings quickly.


 5. Core Gameplay Mechanics
 A. Parliamentary tasks
 Government players receive a list of tasks distributed around Parliament. Completing a task increases a shared
 Government task-progress meter. Deep State players can pretend to perform tasks, but their interactions do not
 increase the meter.

  Task                           Location                         Simple interaction

  Approve the Budget             Finance Ministry                 Click documents in the correct order.

  Pass the Bill                  PM Office                        Complete a short sequence puzzle.

  Organize Files                 Records Room                     Match documents to folders.

  Restore Power                  Central Hall                     Connect matching wire ends.

  Verify Records                 Records Room                     Find matching document IDs.

  Repair Communications          Central Hall                     Complete a simple circuit puzzle.

 For the eight-hour build, implement only three task types. If time is short, a task can simply require a
 player to interact and wait a few seconds. The shared progress meter should be visible to everyone.

 B. Sabotage
 Deep State players can trigger a small set of disruptive events. Start with two if development time is tight; add the
 third only after the main loop works.

  Sabotage                          Effect

  Power Cut                         Temporarily reduces visibility or darkens the map.

 5. Core Gameplay Mechanics (continued)
 C. Elimination
 Deep State agents can eliminate nearby Government MPs. For the MVP, make this an instant action rather than
 building an attack animation.

  Parameter                                       Suggested value

  Elimination range                               Within 1 tile or a small radius

  Kill cooldown                                   25 seconds

  Body reporting                                  Any living player near the body

  Eliminated player                               Movement and voting disabled

 When a player is eliminated, leave a body marker on the map. A nearby player can report it, which starts an
 emergency meeting. Ensure the server validates the target, range, role and cooldown.

 D. Emergency meetings & voting
 An emergency meeting can begin when a player reports a body or uses a meeting button. Limit emergency calls with
 a cooldown or a small number of calls per player.

  Phase                      What happens

  1. Meeting                 All living players enter the meeting screen. Movement and actions pause.

  2. Discussion              Players get 30–45 seconds to accuse, defend themselves and share information. Use text chat;
                             voice chat is out of scope.

  3. Voting                  Each living player selects one player or Skip Vote. Each player gets one vote.

  4. Results                 The player with the most votes is removed. A tie means nobody is removed. Reveal the eliminated
                             player's role.

  5. Resume                  If no faction has won, return players to the map and resume the match.

 Government UI: task list, shared task progress, report button and emergency meeting button. Deep State UI:

 7. Match Lifecycle & Win Conditions
  Stage                         Description

  Lobby                         Players join using a room code. Host starts when ready.

  Role assignment               Server randomly assigns factions and privately sends role information.

  Playing                       Players move around Parliament, complete tasks, sabotage and investigate.

  Meeting / Voting              Players discuss and vote when a body is reported or a meeting is called.

  Game over                     Server checks victory conditions and sends everyone to the results screen.

 Government wins when all shared tasks are complete OR all Deep State agents have been voted out.
 Deep State wins when the number of living Deep State agents is equal to or greater than the number
 of living Government players. Check these conditions after task completion, elimination and voting.

 8. Breaking News System
 A small news ticker gives the game a distinctive political-thriller identity. Whenever a major event occurs, display a
 short message at the top of every player's screen.

 Example headlines
 • BREAKING: Parliament's electricity supply has been compromised!
 • BREAKING: An MP has been found dead in the Finance Ministry!
 • BREAKING: Emergency session called in Lok Sabha!
 • BREAKING: Parliament has voted out a sitting MP!
 • BREAKING: Government operations are back online!

 Implementation: each important server-side game event emits a message; clients display the message for a few
 seconds. This is a relatively inexpensive polish feature.


 9. End-Game Experience
 Avoid a generic victory screen. Make the result feel like the end of a political drama.

 Government victory — “Democracy survives!”
 The Deep State conspiracy has been exposed. Parliament remains operational.

 Deep State victory — “The government has fallen!”
 Parliament has been infiltrated. The government has lost control.

 Final reveal screen should show every player's character, faction and role, tasks completed, eliminations, meetings
 attended and the winning team. This creates a satisfying conclusion and gives spectators a clear demo payoff.


 10. Technical Architecture
 Reuse the existing multiplayer prototype and networking architecture. Do not rewrite working
 networking code during the hackathon.

  Layer                               Responsibilities

  Player browsers                     Render the map and UI, capture keyboard input, display events and send player actions.

  Multiplayer server                  Synchronize players and broadcast authoritative game events.

  Game state                          Track player positions, roles, task progress, cooldowns, bodies, voting state and match status.

 The server must be authoritative for role assignment, kill validation, task completion, voting results and win
 conditions. Clients should request actions; the server validates and broadcasts accepted state changes.

 Suggested game states
 WAITING → ROLE_ASSIGNMENT → PLAYING → MEETING → VOTING → RESULTS
 If no winner is determined after RESULTS, return to PLAYING. If a victory condition is met, transition
 to GAME_OVER.

 12. Scope Control: Build Now vs. Later
 Must build for the MVP
 • Multiplayer lobby with room code.
 • 6–10 players and random role assignment.
 • One connected Parliament map and 2D movement.
 • Government task list and shared progress meter.
 • At least one Deep State sabotage action.
 • Proximity-based elimination and body reporting.
 • Emergency meeting, discussion timer and voting.
 • Victory conditions and final role reveal.

 Explicitly defer until after the hackathon
 • Journalists, lobbyists and additional factions.
 • Private messaging and complex alliances.
 • Bribery, blackmail and individual secret agendas.
 • Evidence collection, fake documents and scandal publishing.
 • Election campaigns and parliamentary bill simulations.
 • Multiple maps, progression systems and character abilities.
 • Voice chat and elaborate animations.

 If development falls behind, simplify task interactions first. A task can be a short interaction with a
 progress timer. Do not remove multiplayer, meetings or voting: those are the heart of the experience.


 13. Demo Checklist
 Before presenting, verify the following with multiple browser windows or devices:

 ■ 6 or more players can join the same room.
 ■ Roles are private and correctly assigned.
 ■ Deep State teammates can identify one another.
 ■ Movement is synchronized for all players.
 ■ Government task progress updates consistently.
 ■ Deep State can sabotage and eliminate within range.
 ■ A body can be reported.
 ■ Meeting pauses gameplay and voting works.
 ■ Ties and skipped votes are handled correctly.
 ■ Both factions can win under the correct conditions.
 ■ Results reveal all roles and the winning faction.


 14. Final Product Pitch
 NETAGIRI: Parliament Under Siege is a multiplayer social-deduction game set inside an Indian
 Parliament. Players take on the roles of ordinary MPs or undercover Deep State agents. While
 Government members work together to complete parliamentary operations, infiltrators secretly
 sabotage the system and eliminate politicians. Through emergency sessions, accusations and
 voting, players must figure out who is working for the country and who is secretly trying to destroy it.

 Product identity
 Genre: Multiplayer social deduction
 Players: 6–10
 Setting: Fictional Indian Parliament
